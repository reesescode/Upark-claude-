---
name: Onboarding eligibility
description: Why the tutorial distinguishes new accounts from older accounts that never received a completion marker.
---

Do not advance the original onboarding account-creation cutoff with future releases. An account without a completion marker is not necessarily a new account.

**Why:** Existing users predate the tutorial and had no completion marker. Gating solely on its absence unexpectedly forced returning users through onboarding; shifting the cutoff later would also skip eligible accounts that had not finished it yet.

**How to apply:** Keep the fixed rollout boundary for automatic prompts. Completion can still be recorded per user, and returning users can reopen the tutorial intentionally from preferences.
